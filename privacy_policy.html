Privacy Policy

Welcome to Myanmar Landslide Division (M.L.D.). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit our website or use its services. We respect your privacy and aim to be clear about how information is managed.

Website privacy information

Myanmar Landslide Division · M.L.D.

01

1. Introduction

Myanmar Landslide Division (M.L.D.) is a digital platform designed to provide landslide-related information, hazard awareness, emergency service details, and disaster preparedness resources across Myanmar.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when users visit the website, submit reports, or use available features.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

02

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, M.L.D. may collect information that users voluntarily provide through website forms.

Examples of information may include:

  • Name or username.
  • Email address and contact details.
  • Location details provided in disaster reports.
  • Descriptions of landslide incidents or hazards.
  • Messages and enquiries submitted through contact forms.

Some basic technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, may also be recorded by website hosting or analytics services if those services are used.

03

3. How We Use Information

Information provided by users may be used to support the operation and improvement of the M.L.D. platform.

Possible uses include:

  • Responding to questions and enquiries.
  • Reviewing submitted landslide and hazard reports.
  • Providing relevant disaster-related information.
  • Improving website features and user experience.
  • Supporting disaster awareness and preparedness activities.

Information should only be used for purposes connected to the services and functions of the platform.

04

4. Location Information

Location information may be provided by users when submitting a landslide report or using location-related website features.

This information can help identify reported hazard locations and provide more relevant geographical information.

If a website feature requests access to your device location, your browser or device may ask for permission. You can manage or deny this permission through your device or browser settings.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal location details when they are not relevant to a report or enquiry.

05

5. Information Sharing

M.L.D. does not intend to make users' personal information publicly available without an appropriate reason.

Information may need to be shared with relevant organisations or service providers when necessary to process enquiries, review disaster reports, or support related services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable laws or lawful requests from relevant authorities.

Users should understand that information submitted to external websites or services is handled according to those services' own privacy policies.

06

6. Data Security

Protecting user information is important. Appropriate measures should be taken to help prevent unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure of information.

However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal details through public forms or messages.

07

7. Data Retention

Information submitted through the website may be kept for as long as necessary to handle enquiries, review reports, support platform functions, or meet relevant legal requirements.

Information that is no longer needed should be reviewed and handled appropriately.

The actual retention period may depend on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

08

8. User Responsibilities

Users are encouraged to provide accurate information when submitting forms, reports, or enquiries.

Users should only submit personal information that is necessary for the service they are using.

Users should not share another person's private information without an appropriate reason or permission.

If you have concerns about information you have submitted, you may contact the M.L.D. team for assistance.

09

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

M.L.D. may update this Privacy Policy when website features, services, or information-handling practices change.

Updated versions will be published on this page. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed.

Continued use of the website after changes are published means that users acknowledge the updated policy.

Important notice
Please avoid submitting passwords, financial information, or unnecessary sensitive personal details through this website. This Privacy Policy describes the intended handling of information and does not guarantee that every website feature or data-handling process is currently active.

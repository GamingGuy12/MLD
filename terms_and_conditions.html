Website terms of use

Myanmar Landslide Division · M.L.D.

01

1. Scope of Services

Myanmar Landslide Division provides a digital platform designed to share landslide-related information, provide access to emergency services, and help users understand potential landslide risks across Myanmar.

The website may include landslide monitoring information, hazard maps, location-based information, risk dashboards, emergency contacts, hospital directories, evacuation shelter information, and disaster reporting services.

The platform is intended to support public awareness and disaster preparedness. It does not replace professional geological investigations, official government announcements, or emergency response organisations.

02

2. User Responsibilities

Users must provide accurate and truthful information when submitting reports, requesting assistance, or using any forms available on the platform.

Users must not submit false reports, misleading information, harmful content, or information intended to disrupt the operation of the website.

Users are responsible for using the platform appropriately and must not attempt to access restricted areas, interfere with website functions, or misuse information provided by the system.

03

3. Information and Accuracy

M.L.D. aims to provide useful and understandable information about landslide risks, affected locations, hazard warnings, and disaster preparedness.

However, landslide conditions can change due to rainfall, soil conditions, environmental changes, and other natural factors. Information displayed on the website may not always reflect the latest conditions.

Users should check relevant official announcements and local authorities before making important decisions based on information provided by the platform.

04

4. Privacy and Security

M.L.D. respects the privacy of users who access the website and submit information through its available forms.

Information such as names, email addresses, reported locations, and assistance requests may be collected when users voluntarily submit forms.

Such information should only be used for purposes related to responding to enquiries, processing submitted reports, improving services, and supporting disaster-related communication.

Users should avoid sharing passwords, financial information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information through public forms.

05

5. Emergency Services and Safety

The emergency services section provides information intended to help users locate emergency contacts, hospitals, evacuation centres, disaster management offices, and humanitarian assistance.

Contact details and locations displayed through external services may change. Users should confirm important information with the relevant organisations before travelling.

The platform does not guarantee that every listed hospital, shelter, or emergency facility is currently open, available, or suitable for every situation.

During an emergency, users should follow instructions from qualified emergency responders and local authorities. The website must not be treated as a replacement for immediate emergency assistance.

06

6. Service Availability

M.L.D. aims to keep its website and digital services accessible to users. However, temporary interruptions may occur because of maintenance, technical problems, internet connectivity, server issues, or circumstances beyond the platform's control.

The website may be updated, modified, or temporarily unavailable when necessary to improve its functionality, security, and overall performance.

M.L.D. does not guarantee uninterrupted access to all website features at all times.

07

7. Copyright and Ownership

Website materials, including text, graphics, logos, icons, layouts, and original designs, are associated with Myanmar Landslide Division unless otherwise stated.

Users may access website content for personal, educational, and informational purposes.

Reproducing, distributing, modifying, or using website materials for commercial purposes without appropriate permission is not permitted.

08

8. Limitation of Liability

M.L.D. provides landslide-related information and disaster assistance resources for general informational purposes. The platform cannot guarantee that all information is completely accurate, current, or available at all times.

M.L.D. is not responsible for decisions made solely on the basis of information displayed on the website, delays caused by external services, inaccurate information submitted by users, or interruptions resulting from technical issues.

Users should always consider official warnings, professional advice, and instructions from emergency authorities when responding to landslide risks or other dangerous situations.

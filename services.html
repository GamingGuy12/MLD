Stay Informed. Stay Safe.

Landslides can happen with little warning, especially after prolonged or heavy rainfall. The Myanmar Landslide Division helps communities understand landslide hazards, explore risk information, and learn practical ways to prepare. Use our map and monitoring dashboard to stay aware of conditions in landslide-prone areas.
